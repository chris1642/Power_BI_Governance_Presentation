The Hidden Risk

But then it gets complicated…

  • Change a measure → broken visual
  • Rename a column → bookmarks fail
  • Delete a table → pages crash
Reality
One small update can silently break dozens of visuals across multiple workspaces — and you won't know until users complain.
~20 reports · ~250 pages — all from a single edit.
